### Notes for the owner
- **Coworking** applied only to genuine laptop-friendly cafes on the "Cafe for working" list. It was deliberately NOT applied to Mamá Mía, Mi Bistro 300, or Zibu Allende, which appear on that list but are full restaurants/nightlife, not work cafes.
- **Groups** applied where the venue is clearly a gathering space (big patio, casona, courtyard + live music, rooftop, food hall), not to every family-friendly spot.
- **Datenight** applied to places on the "Date night" list or with a documented romantic reputation.
