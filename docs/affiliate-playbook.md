# Vamos San Miguel: Affiliate Program Signup Playbook

A practical, step-by-step guide for monetizing a bilingual travel and relocation guide for San Miguel de Allende (SMA). Every commission rate and program detail below was checked against a page that was opened during research. Where a number could not be confirmed on an official page, it is explicitly flagged as "unverified" or "from third-party sources."

**How to read this doc**
- Rates and terms change often. Re-check them at signup.
- "Verified" means confirmed on the merchant's own official page. "Third-party / unverified" means the figure comes from affiliate directories or search summaries, not a merchant page we opened.
- No figure below was invented. When we could not confirm, we say so.

## 1. Viator

**What it is and why it fits SMA.** Viator (a Tripadvisor company) is one of the largest marketplaces for tours, activities, and experiences. San Miguel de Allende is saturated with bookable experiences: walking tours, mezcal and wine tastings, hot-air balloon rides, day trips to Guanajuato and Dolores Hidalgo. This is the single most natural fit for a travel guide.

**Commission (verified).** 8% on completed bookings. High-performing affiliates may reach up to 12% (the 12% figure is from third-party reviews, not the official page). Source: Viator Partner Resource Center.

**Cookie window (verified).** 30 days. Commission pays out only after the experience has been completed. Source: Viator Partner Resource Center.

**Signup / approval (verified).** "Quick and free sign-up with no traffic or follower minimums." Signup is fast if you already have a Tripadvisor account. Payment is weekly via PayPal (no minimum) or monthly via bank transfer ($50 minimum). Source: Viator Partner Resource Center.

**Start here:** Go to https://www.viator.com/partner/affiliates (or partnerresources.viator.com), create/link a Tripadvisor account, and complete the application form. You can be live the same day.

## 2. Wise

**What it is and why it fits SMA.** Wise (formerly TransferWise) handles low-cost international money transfers and multi-currency accounts. This is arguably the highest-intent product for the relocation half of the audience: anyone moving to or living in SMA needs to move USD/CAD/EUR into MXN, pay Mexican rent, or hold multiple currencies. Very warm audience.

**Commission (partially verified).** The official Wise Partner onboarding guide confirms a CPA (cost-per-acquisition) model paid for each new paying customer, but does **not** publish the dollar amount. Third-party directories report roughly £10 per personal-account referral and £50 per business referral. Treat those amounts as **unverified**; confirm the current CPA inside the partner dashboard. Source: Wise Partner onboarding guide.

**Cookie window (verified).** 30-day conversion window for the initial referral, plus a one-year window during which repeat conversions from a clicked user are credited. Source: Wise Partner onboarding guide.

**Signup / approval (verified).** You must have a Wise account and use your in-app referral link for social/SEO/commercial promotion. For a formal affiliate/CPA partnership you email partnerwise@wise.com. Wise reviews each partnership after 6 months with scope to raise the rate. Source: Wise Partner onboarding guide.

**Start here:** Open a Wise account, grab the in-app referral link for immediate use, then email partnerwise@wise.com to apply for the CPA affiliate partnership.

## 3. Discover Cars

**What it is and why it fits SMA.** Discover Cars is a car-rental comparison and booking site. SMA has no commercial airport of its own (arrivals come through Querétaro, Guanajuato/Bajío, or Mexico City) and readers frequently rent cars to reach town and explore the wider Bajío. Good, practical fit.

**Commission (verified).** 70% of Discover Cars' car-rental profit plus 30% of Full Coverage revenue, which they say averages about $20 per booking. Note this is a share of their profit, not of the total rental price. Source: Discover Cars affiliate page.

**Cookie window (verified).** 365 days, the longest window of any program in this doc. Source: Discover Cars affiliate page.

**Signup / approval (verified).** Free and takes a few minutes. You provide name, a username (affiliate ID), email, and a website URL, then accept the terms. Payouts go to bank or PayPal on the 10th of the following month after rentals complete. Source: Discover Cars affiliate page and affiliate terms.

**Start here:** Apply at https://www.discovercars.com/affiliate. A website URL is requested at signup, so have your domain ready even if the site is minimal.

## 4. SafetyWing

**What it is and why it fits SMA.** SafetyWing sells travel medical and nomad health insurance aimed at remote workers and long-term travelers. Strong fit for the younger digital-nomad and part-year relocator segment that SMA attracts.

**Commission (partially verified).** The official SafetyWing Ambassador page states "a flat affiliate fee (currently benchmarked at approximately 10% of total premium amount)" on completed applications through your link. The official page does **not** state the recurring duration. Third-party sources report the 10% recurs on renewals for up to 12 months / 364 days; treat the recurring duration as **unverified**. Source: SafetyWing Ambassador page.

**Cookie window.** Not stated on the official ambassador page we opened. Third-party sources cite a 30-day cookie; treat as **unverified**.

**Signup / approval.** "Register in under 5 minutes" via the ambassador program; no purchase required. Third-party reviews say approval typically takes under a week. Source: SafetyWing Ambassador page.

**Start here:** Apply at https://safetywing.com/ambassador and describe your site/content in the form.

## 5. GetYourGuide

**What it is and why it fits SMA.** GetYourGuide is a major tours-and-activities marketplace, a direct complement to Viator. Running both lets you compare inventory and pick whichever lists a given SMA experience. Same strong activity fit.

**Commission (third-party / not confirmed on official page).** Widely reported at 8% per sale (some networks such as Awin list a 7% base). The official partner site is a JavaScript app that did not expose the rate to our fetch tool, so treat 8% as **unverified pending confirmation in the partner dashboard**. Sources: getlasso and other affiliate directories; official partner site could not be read directly.

**Cookie window (third-party).** Reported 30-day attribution; commissions calculated monthly on completed bookings and paid by bank transfer or PayPal. Treat as **unverified**. Source: affiliate directories.

**Signup / approval.** Apply through the GetYourGuide partner/creator program. Applications are reviewed (typically a few days) and consider your site and audience, so this fits better once content is live. Source: GetYourGuide partner site (partner.getyourguide.com).

**Start here:** Apply at https://partner.getyourguide.com after your site has a few published SMA activity pages. Confirm the exact commission and cookie in the dashboard before publishing rate claims.

## 6. Booking.com

**What it is and why it fits SMA.** Booking.com is the dominant accommodation platform. SMA has heavy demand for hotels, boutique stays, and short-term rentals, so lodging content converts well. This is the accommodation anchor of the program.

**Commission (verified via CJ).** Through the CJ network listing, Booking.com pays 4% for completed accommodation stays, 6% for completed car rentals, 4% for completed attractions, 4% for completed taxi rides, and £2/€2 per flight. Rates may rise with performance/volume. Source: CJ Booking.com program page.

Note: separate travel-agent/partner framing is sometimes described as "25% to 40%," but that is a share of Booking's own commission, a different model, and comes from third-party sources. The 4%/6% figures above are the ones we verified on the CJ page.

**Cookie window (verified via CJ).** Session-based. The reader must complete the booking in the same browser session as the click. No multi-day cookie. This makes conversion timing tighter than Viator or Discover Cars. Source: CJ Booking.com program page.

**Signup / approval (needs a live site).** Requires an active website, blog, or social profile, and Booking.com explicitly will **not** accept social-media-only publishers. You select a region and complete the application. Source: Booking.com/CJ program materials.

**Start here:** After the site is live with lodging content, apply either directly at https://partnerships.booking.com or through CJ (https://www.cj.com). Direct signup keeps you on Booking's own terms; CJ bundles reporting with other merchants.

## 7. Amazon Associates

**What it is and why it fits SMA.** Amazon's affiliate program pays on nearly anything a reader buys after clicking. For a relocation guide, this suits "what to pack / what to buy for your move" content. Fit is moderate: rates are low and a Mexico-based audience may shop on Amazon Mexico (amazon.com.mx has its own separate Associates program) rather than the US store.

**Commission (third-party; official rate card is login-gated).** Amazon's official fee schedule sits behind a login, so we could not open it directly. Reputable summaries report a range of roughly 1% to 10% for most categories, with Amazon Games around 20% as an outlier; furniture/home improvement dropped to 3% in 2020; everyday goods (grocery, electronics, home) sit at the low 1% to 4% end. Treat exact per-category numbers as **unverified against Amazon's own page**; confirm in your Associates dashboard. Source: Geniuslink summary of Amazon rates.

**Cookie window (third-party).** 24-hour click window: the reader must add an item to cart within 24 hours of the click. Once in the cart, attribution holds for 90 days. Source: Geniuslink summary.

**Signup / approval (needs a live site + fast sales).** You need a qualifying website or app, and Amazon requires at least 3 qualifying sales within 180 days of signup or the account is closed. Because of that clock, apply only once the site has traffic that can convert. Decide up front whether to target the US store or amazon.com.mx based on where your readers actually buy.

**Start here:** After the site is live and getting visitors, apply at https://affiliate-program.amazon.com (and/or the Mexico program at afiliados.amazon.com.mx). Confirm current category rates in the dashboard before publishing any numbers.

## 8. Cigna Global and/or IMG Global (expat health insurance)

**What it is and why it fits SMA.** Both sell international/expat health insurance. This is the highest-value-per-sale category for the relocation audience: older, full-time relocators to SMA who want comprehensive international coverage. One conversion can be worth far more than a tour booking. The tradeoff is a longer approval path and opaque public rates.

### Cigna Global
**Commission (not publicly disclosed).** The official Cigna Global affiliates page states only that "partnerships can be adapted to meet both Cigna and partner's needs in terms of commissions" and that "you'll receive fees due automatically each month." No rate is published. Third-party directories report roughly £8 per sale or £10 per quote; treat these as **unverified**. Source: Cigna Global affiliates signup page; third-party directories (FlexOffers, TapRefer) for the amounts.

**Network.** Reported to run on Impact.com (third-party); the official page shows a direct email-based signup form (region selection: APAC, Europe, Global, UAE, USA).

**Start here:** Register at https://www.cignaglobal.com/affiliates/sign-up, select your region, and request the current commission terms from the affiliate team.

### IMG Global (International Medical Group)
**Commission (not publicly disclosed).** The official IMG affiliate page lists benefits ("unlimited earning potential," "no minimum payout threshold") but publishes **no** commission rate or cookie window. Source: IMG affiliate program page.

**Network (verified).** Runs on Impact.com. You must create a free Impact account and accept the IMG Referral Marketing Agreement to apply. Source: IMG affiliate program page.

**Start here:** Apply through Impact via https://www.imglobal.com/affiliate-program. For specifics, the page directs questions to IMG's communications manager.

**Recommendation for this category:** Because both hide their rates and lean toward established publishers, pursue them AFTER the site has demonstrable relocation traffic. Apply to whichever responds with the better disclosed terms; there is no need to run both at first.
