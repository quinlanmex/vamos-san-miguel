# Getting To and Around San Miguel de Allende

The fastest way in is to fly into Queretaro (QRO), the closest airport, and drive or shuttle about an hour to town. Leon/Bajio (BJX) is the next closest at roughly 90 minutes to two hours and often has more flight options. Once you arrive, you will barely need a vehicle. The historic center is compact and walkable, and taxis and Uber handle everything else. This guide covers airports, buses, and getting around on the ground.

## Which airport is closest to San Miguel de Allende?

Queretaro International Airport (QRO) is the closest, about a 1 hour to 1 hour 30 minute drive of roughly 50 miles, with simple, well-maintained highway most of the way. Leon/Bajio International Airport (BJX) near Leon is next, about 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours and roughly 60 miles, and it often has a wider choice of international flights. Mexico City International Airport (MEX) is the farthest at roughly 3 to 4 hours by road, but it offers the most connections and is a common entry point.

Pick QRO for the shortest, most predictable transfer. Pick BJX when it saves you a connection or fits your flights better. Use MEX when it is the only sensible international routing, and plan to continue by bus or private transfer.

## How do I get from the airport to town?

From QRO and BJX your main options are:

- **Private transfer.** Fastest and most comfortable, booked ahead and waiting at arrivals. Best for late-night landings and groups.
- **Shared shuttle.** The most economical door-to-door option. Reserve a couple of days ahead.
- **Rental car.** Useful only if you plan several countryside day trips, since you will not need it inside town.
- **Bus.** Possible with a connection through the nearest city's bus terminal, and most direct from Mexico City.

Book shuttles a few days in advance and lock in private transfers when you book your flight, especially around festivals and for late arrivals. See our Local Picks for transfer suggestions.

## Can I take a bus to San Miguel de Allende?

Yes, and it is a comfortable, popular way to arrive. Mexico's first-class bus lines, including Primera Plus and ETN, serve San Miguel's Central de Autobuses (the town bus station), which sits a short taxi ride from the center.

From Mexico City, buses leave from Terminal Central de Autobuses del Norte (Terminal Norte) and take roughly 3 hours 50 minutes to 4 hours. From Queretaro, the ride is short, around 1 hour 30 minutes. First-class Mexican buses are clean and air-conditioned with assigned seats, and you can book online in advance or at the terminal.

## How do I get around once I am there?

**On foot.** The historic center is about 1.5 kilometers across, and you can reach most sights within 20 to 30 minutes of the Jardin. This is how you will spend most of your time. Wear shoes with grip, since the streets are cobblestone and hilly and turn slippery after rain.

**Uber.** Uber operates in San Miguel and is a reliable, low-cost way to reach the hot springs, Fabrica La Aurora, outer neighborhoods, and the bus station.

**Taxis.** Local taxis are easy to find around the Jardin and other busy spots. Agree on the fare before you set off, since meters are not standard.

## Do I need a car in San Miguel de Allende?

For a town-focused trip, no. Walking plus the occasional taxi or Uber covers everything, and cobblestone streets, hills, and tight parking make driving in the center more hassle than help. A rental car only earns its keep if you plan to string together several countryside day trips on your own schedule.

## Frequently asked questions

**Should I fly into QRO or BJX?**
QRO is closer and gives the shortest, most predictable drive of about an hour. BJX is a bit farther but often has more flight options. Choose whichever fits your flights and budget best.

**How long is the bus from Mexico City?**
Plan for roughly 3 hours 50 minutes to 4 hours on a first-class line like Primera Plus or ETN from Terminal Norte, traffic depending.

**Is Uber available in San Miguel de Allende?**
Yes. Uber runs reliably in town and is handy for trips beyond the walkable center, like the hot springs or the bus station.

**Is San Miguel de Allende walkable?**
Very. The center is compact, around 1.5 kilometers across, and most sights are a 20 to 30 minute walk from the Jardin. Just plan for cobblestones and hills.

**How far is San Miguel from Mexico City by car?**
Roughly 3 to 4 hours by road, depending heavily on traffic leaving and entering the capital.

**Can I get from the airport to town late at night?**
Yes, with a pre-booked private transfer. Arrange it when you book your flight so a driver is waiting at arrivals, since late-night shared shuttles and buses are limited.
