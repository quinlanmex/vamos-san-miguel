# Cost of Living in San Miguel de Allende for a Couple: A Monthly Budget

A couple can live comfortably in San Miguel de Allende for roughly US$1,800 to $3,500 a month, all in, well under what the same life costs in most U.S. metros. That range is wide on purpose, because your neighborhood and your habits move it more than anything else. This page breaks a two-person budget down category by category so you can build your own number instead of trusting a headline.

Figures are approximate, in USD, and dated to mid-2026. Peso amounts convert at roughly 18 to 20 pesos per dollar, and the exchange rate is a hidden variable in your real dollar cost, so budget with a little cushion.

## What does it cost for a couple to live in San Miguel de Allende?

Plan on US$1,800 to $3,500 a month for two people living comfortably, with housing as the biggest swing. A couple who rents a one-bedroom near the Centro Histórico, eats out a few times a week, and keeps a weekly housekeeper lands in the upper half of that range. A couple in a two-bedroom outside Centro who cooks most nights lands lower. Here is a representative monthly budget.

| Category | Approx. monthly (USD) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rent, 1BR furnished in or near Centro | $900 to $1,500 | Two-bedroom outside Centro runs $700 to $1,300 |
| Utilities (electric, gas, water) | $50 to $120 | Little to no AC needed at altitude |
| Home internet (fiber) | $20 to $40 | Often faster than U.S. equivalents |

| Transport, phone, incidentals | $100 to $200 | San Miguel is walkable; a car is optional |
| **Rough total** | **$1,980 to $3,570** | Comfortable in the middle; lean lower by moving out from Centro |

## How much is rent for a couple?

Rent is the single biggest line, and it tracks almost entirely to how close you want to be to the Centro Histórico. A one-bedroom in the walkable heart of town runs roughly US$700 to $1,200 a month unfurnished, and a two-bedroom in or near Centro commonly lands between $1,300 and $2,000. Step out to San Antonio, Guadalupe, or La Lejona and the same money buys more space. Furnished costs more, usually a premium of about US$115 to $340 a month over the unfurnished version of the same place. Standard practice is one month's deposit plus first month's rent upfront.

## What do groceries and dining cost for two?

Budget roughly US$400 to $650 a month on groceries for a couple, plus whatever you spend eating out. Fresh produce, meat, dairy, and tortillas from the Tuesday market and local shops run dramatically cheaper than in the U.S. and taste better for the short trip they made. Imports carry the burden of distance and tariffs, so electronics, clothing, and packaged goods often cost more than back home. A mid-range meal out for two runs about US$40 to $50, while the inexpensive spots come closer to US$15 to $20. For big-box shopping, Querétaro is about an hour away with Costco and Sam's Club, and many couples make a monthly run.

## How much should a couple budget for healthcare?

Carry real coverage rather than going bare, and pay cash for routine care. A general-practitioner visit in San Miguel runs roughly US$25 to $50, and a specialist US$50 to $100, often less than a U.S. copay before anyone touches a deductible. For the bigger events, private health insurance commonly runs US$1,500 to $3,000 a year per person depending on age and health history, so a couple should budget roughly US$250 to $500 a month between two policies. For anything specialized, the larger city of Querétaro is about an hour away with full hospitals.

## Can domestic help fit a couple's budget?

Yes, and the math is what makes it reachable. Housekeeping runs roughly 60 to 90 pesos an hour, about US$3 to $5, with a full day landing around US$25 to $40. For the price of a single U.S. deep clean, a couple can have help every week for a month. Budget one obligation that is easy to miss: Mexican labor law requires an aguinaldo, a holiday bonus of at least 15 days' wages, paid by December 20, and it covers household staff. Treat it as a real cost, not a tip.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is US$2,000 a month enough for a couple in San Miguel?** It is workable for a couple who rents outside the immediate Centro, cooks most nights, and keeps dining and help modest. Prime Centro rent and frequent dining push a comfortable budget toward US$2,500 or more.

**Is San Miguel expensive compared to other Mexican towns?** Yes, relatively. It is one of Mexico's most established expat towns, so you pay a premium for plug-and-play infrastructure and a ready-made community. Even with that premium, a couple lives well for a fraction of a U.S. metro.

**Do we need a car?** Not in the walkable core. Many couples skip a car and use taxis and the occasional rental for Querétaro runs. A car buys convenience, not necessity.
